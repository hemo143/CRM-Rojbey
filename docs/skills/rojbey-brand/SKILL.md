# Rojbey — Brand & Content Skill

Rojbey is an İstanbul couture house that chose Cairo: Turkish-made luxury wedding gowns (for purchase or rental), fitted in a private atelier at **22 Almaza St., Cairo**. Bridal only — never evening wear.

Everything the brand makes rests on one idea: **effort observed becomes value felt** (the labour illusion). When a bride watches the hours, the beads and the patient hands, she re-prices the gown upward without a price ever being mentioned. So every piece of content and every design must make **effort, care and rarity visible** — and then stay quiet.

Read this whole file before writing or designing. At the end, run the checklist in section 12.

## 4. Tone of voice

**We speak the way the designer speaks in the atelier: softly, with confidence, with precise detail. Showing is enough.**

- **Quiet, never loud** — هادئة، لا صاخبة. Short sentences. No exclamation marks, no all-caps, no sales shouting.
- **Precise, never vague** — دقيقة، لا عامة. A number or detail instead of an adjective: "190 hours", not "high quality".
- **Intimate, never familiar** — قريبة، لا مُتبسّطة. Address her directly, with respect and graceful distance. Never "girls" or pet names.
- **Proud, never boastful** — فخورة، لا متباهية. Show the work, let her judge. No "the best", "number one", or comparisons.

Tone scale: closer to formal than casual · serious more than playful · reserved more than expressive · traditional with a modern edge.

**Language by space**
| Space | Language | Example |
|---|---|---|
| Brand lines & slogans | English + elegant Modern Standard Arabic | ثمينة في كل تفاصيلها. |
| Captions & stories | Simple, refined Arabic with English terms like Fitting, Couture | الـ Fitting الثاني... والكُمّ يحتاج لمسة أخيرة. |
| Video dialogue | Natural Egyptian Arabic, with written subtitles | خطوة واحدة لقدّام... من غير ما تبصّي. |
| Gulf content | Light, warm Modern Standard Arabic | نضبط فستانكِ بدقة... حتى عن بُعد. |

## 5. The eight writing rules

1. **One idea per post.** Two ideas = two posts.
2. **Lead with a number or a detail.** "8,400 beads" beats "a stunning gown". Never open with an adjective.
3. **Show, don't claim.** Tell what actually happened in the atelier.
4. **Short sentences, full stops.** The full stop is confidence. Zero exclamation marks.
5. **Speak to her.** One woman, directly — not "girls", not "all brides".
6. **Name a person.** Nadia, the Designer, the bride of the season.
7. **End on something unfinished.** "On Thursday, we'll show you why."
8. **Zero emojis, exactly three hashtags:** `#Rojbey #RojbeyBride #RojbeyCouture`

**Before / after**
- ✗ OMG this dress is EVERYTHING!!! Best bridal quality in Egypt, DM for prices 💍
- ✓ Three fittings in, and the sleeve is being remade. Nadia says it isn't right yet. On Thursday, we'll show you why.
- ✗ عرض خاص!!🔥 فساتين زفاف تركي بأسعار ولا في الأحلام، الحقي احجزي قبل ما العرض يخلص!!!✨
- ✓ ١٩٠ ساعة من العمل. ٨٤٠٠ خرزة. وفستان واحد تسكن قيمته في كل تفصيلة. مواعيد الـ Fitting الخاصة لموسم الربيع متاحة الآن.

**Prices — never in captions, comments or designs.** Standard reply to "how much?":
> «تختلف القيمة حسب الفستان، وحسب الشراء أو التأجير. يسعدنا أن نوضّح لكِ كل التفاصيل في موعدك الخاص بالأتيليه.»
> (The value depends on the gown, and on purchase or rental. We'd be glad to share every detail at your private appointment in the atelier.)

**Call to action — always:** Book your private appointment · احجزي موعدك الخاص. Never "order now", never "DM for price", never "drop by".

Purchase and rental are spoken about in the same calm language — the value is in the craft, not the transaction.

## 7. Numbers — the proof rule

The brand promise is that every message is backed by a real, counted number from the atelier (hours, beads, crystals, metres, fittings, weeks, years). Therefore:
- **Never invent a number and present it as fact.** If the user hasn't given the real figure, write it as a placeholder in square brackets — `[8,400] beads`, `[190] hours` — and tell the user to replace every bracket before publishing.
- Numbers from the guidelines (190 hours, 8,400 beads) are illustrative examples, not facts about a specific gown.
- Western numerals in English designs, Arabic-Indic numerals (١٩٠) in Arabic designs — never mixed in one design.
- The number is the hero: one number per design, large, in gold.

**Three moods**
- **Day — "The Bride":** Pearl background. Brides, editorials, craft.

### Logo
- **Primary:** name + flourish + "Wedding Dress" — storefront, stationery, website, introductory ads. Min 160 px / 35 mm.
- **Secondary:** name + flourish only — video end cards, packaging, covers, posts. Min 100 px / 22 mm.
- **Monogram:** letter R inside the diamond frame — profile picture, icons, seals, highlight covers. Min 32 px / 8 mm.
- Approved: Gold on Pearl (primary) · Pearl on Night · Gold on Night · Pearl on a calm photo area.

- ✗ Never: add "Evening Wear" or any category · stretch · recolour · shadows/glows/3D · busy backgrounds · rotate · shiny gold/fake foil backgrounds · attach taglines · weakening transparency.
- **When building a design:** use the real logo file if the user provides one. If not, don't redraw the wordmark — use the monogram (Cormorant "R" inside a thin gold diamond) or a clearly labelled logo placeholder box.

### Graphic elements (quiet, drawn from the logo and the diamond)
- **Facets pattern** — diamond cut / Ottoman geometry, 5–12% opacity on Night or Pearl. Never behind faces or long text.
- **The thread** — one fine curved line from the logo's flourish, linking or separating. One per design.
- **The diamond frame** — for the monogram, episode numbers, highlight covers.
- **The hairline** — 0.5–1 pt gold divider. No thick lines or bars.
- **The number as graphic** — the strongest visual element.
- ✗ Never: stickers, emojis in designs, floral frames, glitter, fake gold gradients, drop shadows, 3D shapes, sale/discount badges, busy arrows/icons, collages.

### Layouts — posts are 4:5, 1080 × 1350 px, 72 px safe margin, 6-column grid
- **Layout A · Full bleed** — photo fills the frame, small logo, **no text on the image**. Brides, editorials.
- **Layout B · Split** — detail photo above, one line below on Pearl (small Jost caps series label + one Cormorant line + gold hairline). Craft and stories.
- **Layout C · Number-led** — large gold number on Night (optional facets), Jost label above, one line explaining it below.

Layout rules: ≥40% empty space · one hero element only (a photo, a number or a sentence) · centred text or one consistent alignment per series · series names in Jost caps at the top · no text over faces or embroidery.

## 9. Photography & video

**Photography** — one feeling: made with care, without hurry.
- Light: soft window light + one small hard source so beads flare. Craft shots: warm 3200K key at 45° with hard backlight (without it, embroidery looks flat).
- Grade: warm, neutral, soft contrast, deep but never crushed blacks, fabric white stays true white, natural skin, minimal retouching. **No filters that change fabric colour** — colour accuracy is part of the value.
- Composition: generous negative space, subject on the third, one idea per image, calm straight angles, room left for text and logo.
- The woman: calm, confident, often looking away, natural movement; hands are the second hero; modest cuts always present in Gulf content.
- Places: the atelier first (worktable, mirror, window light); quiet classical architecture; İstanbul without tourist clichés (no mosque skylines, no Bosphorus bridge).
- ✓ macro beads/threads/needles, light catching crystal, genuine reactions (written consent), shots from behind the mirror.
- ✗ Instagram filters, ring lights, direct flash, busy backgrounds, artificial flowers, shiny décor, body reshaping, heavy skin smoothing, collages.

**The four essential shots:** 01 The Portrait (full bride on the third, 85mm, window light) · 02 The Detail (needle entering tulle, 100mm macro, hard side light) · 03 The Atelier (worktable, hands, tools — same corner every Thursday) · 04 The Reveal (camera behind the mirror, cut on her face, not the gown).

**Video & reels**
- Format: 9:16 with a 4:5 safe zone; reel covers follow post layouts.
- The first second: open on a macro detail — never the logo, never a greeting.
- Pace: shots of 2–6 s; straight cuts or slow dissolves only; no zooms, whooshes or effects.
- Sound: real atelier sound (thread, scissors, fabric, steam, tape measure). Silence welcome. Occasional quiet piano/strings; never music under speech; never trending audio.
- On-screen text: one line, and it carries a number. Jost or IBM Plex Sans Arabic, centred low, fading in/out.
- Dialogue: natural Egyptian Arabic with burned-in subtitles (Gulf content: light MSA).
- The reveal: cut on her face, never the gown.
- End card (last 1.5 s): monogram in the diamond frame on Night + *Precious in every detail.*
- Consent: signed release before any client, mother or guest is filmed. No exceptions.

**Craft-film skeleton (≈25 s):** 0–0.8 s macro needle into tulle (120 fps at 40%) → 0.8–3 s hands at real rhythm → 3–8 s overhead, motif forming → 8–14 s time-lapse across a working day → 14–20 s hard raking light, beads flare → 20–25 s wide, panel on the gown → end card.

## 10. AI video rules (Google Flow · Veo 3.1)

A single invented detail breaks a brand whose promise is value made visible. So:
1. AI never invents a gown, embroidery or a person. Every gown and face comes from a real Rojbey photograph.
2. AI adds motion to real photos (light passing, veil lifting, slow push-in) and creates atmosphere with no product (light, fabric, thread, city, materials).
3. Never generate motion *between two states* of a gown (sketch → toile → gown) — use separate clips joined by straight cuts.
4. Never animate a real bride's face or body; never make people in archive photos move.
5. Check every frame against the source; discard if beads were added, a neckline changed, embroidery softened, text/signs invented, or a figure appeared in a mirror or doorway.
6. Generated audio is a guide track only — replace with real atelier sound. Never ask the model to render text; add text in the edit.
7. Apply the platform's AI-content label.

**Flow modes:** *Frames to Video* (default — anything showing a Rojbey product; real start/end frames) · *Ingredients to Video* (objects that must stay consistent: tea glass, sketchbook, Lunéville hook) · *Text to Video* (atmosphere with no Rojbey product).
**Setup:** one Flow project per piece · 9:16 · drafts on Veo 3.1 Fast (4 per clip), final on Quality with the identical prompt · clips of 4/6/8 s, trimmed to 2–6 s in the edit.

**Prompt anatomy:** shot & lens → subject → action → camera movement → light → setting → mood → the Rojbey look → audio → fidelity. Always end prompts with these two sentences:
> **The Rojbey look:** Style: quiet luxury couture film, warm neutral colour grade, soft contrast, deep blacks that are never crushed, true ivory whites, fine natural film grain, real fabric texture, no filters, no glow, no lens flares.
> **Fidelity:** Keep everything in the reference image exactly as it is — do not add, remove or change any embroidery, beads, seams, neckline, sleeves or silhouette, and do not morph or warp the fabric.

For Text-to-Video atmosphere, replace the fidelity line with explicit exclusions (e.g. "No hands, no people, no text, no readable signs, no landmarks").

## 12. Final checklist — run before delivering anything

**Words**
- [ ] One idea; proves exactly one sub-message; tagged Pillar × Job
- [ ] Opens on a number or concrete detail, not an adjective
- [ ] No exclamation marks, no emojis, no all-caps shouting
- [ ] No banned words (section 6); never "Turkish dress" alone; never evening wear
- [ ] No prices; CTA is "Book your private appointment / احجزي موعدك الخاص"
- [ ] Addresses one woman directly; names a real person where possible
- [ ] Every unverified number is in [brackets]
- [ ] Exactly three hashtags; Arabic then English
- [ ] Ends on an open loop or the booking invitation

**Design**
- [ ] Only Gold / Pearl / Night and their tints; gold used as accent
- [ ] Max two typefaces from the approved four
- [ ] ≥40% empty space; one hero element; no text on faces or embroidery
- [ ] Logo small, centred top or bottom, never altered — or monogram/placeholder
- [ ] 4:5 at 1080 × 1350 with 72 px margins (or 9:16 with 4:5 safe zone)
- [ ] No gradients, shadows, stickers, glitter, badges, emojis

**Video**
- [ ] Opens on macro, ends on the monogram end card (1.5 s)
- [ ] Shots 2–6 s, straight cuts/slow dissolves, real sound, no music under speech
- [ ] One on-screen line carrying a number
- [ ] Consent noted for every real person; AI clips follow section 10

If a request conflicts with the brand (discount post, price in caption, "best in Egypt", trending audio, emoji-heavy copy), don't silently comply — offer the on-brand alternative and briefly say why, then do what the user decides.
